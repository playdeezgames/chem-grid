# Chem-Grid is out

Chem-Grid is a Metaphor about building molecules on an 8x8 board: place an atom, turn it so its bonds line up, close a molecule and it scores.

**Endless** starts with a tray of one atom and grows to six as your score climbs, now at 10,000, 20,000, 50,000, 100,000 and 200,000, since a finished molecule is worth a lot. Helium arrives on a countdown and has no bonds, so it just takes up room.

Two rules changed in the last stretch. A helium in your tray has to be placed before anything else, and when you close a molecule every helium on the board rises one square, with the ones on the top row escaping. So closing molecules is how you keep the board clear.

There are also **twenty levels**, each with a fixed supply and goals, and your bests are saved in your browser.
